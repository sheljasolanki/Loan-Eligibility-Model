# Insights from 10,000 simulated applicants

Generated by `python scripts/simulate.py` (seed 2026). The applicants are **synthetic** (assumption A10): the numbers show how the published rules behave across a plausible salaried population, not how many real applicants each lender approves.

## Headline numbers

- **90%** of applicants are approved by at least one lender; the median applicant gets **6** of 20.
- **Fees change the best lender for 6% of applicants.** When they do, choosing by headline rate costs a median **INR 1,187** more over the same tenure.
- **+50 CIBIL points** unlock **3.95** more lenders on average for applicants scoring 650-749.
- **An SBI salary account** turns SBI from no to yes for **47%** of applicants who don't have one (SBI requires it).
- **Estimated data matters:** with every peer estimate at its strict bound an applicant averages 6.62 approvals, at its lenient bound 9.57 (base 8.27); the answer moves for 76% of applicants.
- **Rate check:** for CIBIL 750+ the model's median HDFC rate is **11.25%**; HDFC's published average actual rate for Apr-Jun 2026 is **10.89%**.
